Neon Bay Heat — a complete 3D open-world action/driving game inspired by GTA 6 (Vice City / Leonida), built with Three.js (vendored, zero network requests). Neon Bay city with beachfront art-deco hotels, a boardwalk and pier, downtown casino row, port and park, day/sunset/night cycle, on-foot play plus 8 vehicles (supercar, muscle car, convertible, van, taxi, police cruiser, armored truck, speedboat) with arcade driving physics, drift and nitro, a 0–5 star wanted system with police pursuit, roadblocks, spike strips and helicopters, 4 weapons, 6 story missions (race, getaway, heist, boat run, time trial, night delivery) plus stunt jumps, taxi fares and 3 shops (AMMO-NATION, MOD SHOP, PAWN SHOP), GTA-style HUD with radar minimap, and 3 procedural Web Audio radio stations. Runs at a fixed 60 Hz timestep with zero console errors.

## Missions (jobs for Lena)

| Job | What you do | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SUNSET SPRINT | Street race, 5 rings down Palma Drive | $1,750 |
| HOT PURSUIT | Hit 3 checkpoints with a 2-star wanted level | $2,000 |
| HARBOR HEIST | Ram the armored truck to a stop, grab the cash, deliver it | $3,500 |
| SMUGGLER'S RUN | Take the boat from the pier, round 5 buoys, come home | $3,000 |
| NIGHT SHIFT | Downtown time trial: 6 rings, 100 seconds | $1,200 |
| NEON RUN | Night delivery: 4 drops across the city | $2,200 |

Plus taxi fares for City Cab, stunt jumps (+$250), and three shops to spend the cash in.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a browser and press PLAY.
